# Content Pillars and Allocation

## Editorial scope

TECHENIGMASTUDIOS covers nine connected areas of technology education. Each pillar can contain foundational explainers, deeper lessons, demonstrations, comparisons, misconceptions, projects, and learning paths.

The initial allocation below is a **planning hypothesis** for a pilot period, not a market-validated permanent split. Creator-OS research and first-party analytics should inform adjustments.

## Allocation rules

- Treat these percentages as an initial editorial planning mix only.
- Review the mix after the first 30-day publishing experiment and at regular intervals.
- Use the shared Creator-OS opportunity-scoring method; do not invent demand evidence to justify a preferred topic.
- One content item must have one primary pillar. Related pillars can be recorded as secondary tags without double-counting the item.
- Fine-tuning is a dedicated learning track that also connects to Generative AI and Deep Learning.
- Feature engineering is a dedicated learning track that also connects to Machine Learning and Data Science.
- Career roadmaps should be role-specific and evidence-backed, not generic lists of technologies.
- Keep the nine pillars stable unless an explicit brand decision changes them. Market research should improve priority, packaging, examples, and formats—not silently replace the niche.

## Long-term lesson-library ambition

The long-term ambition is approximately **250–300 distinct lesson candidates per pillar**. That is a curriculum research target, not an obligation to publish that number.

Before a candidate becomes a lesson, it must have:
- A distinct audience question or learning objective.
- A place in the learning sequence.
- A useful explanation, example, or demonstration.
- Evidence and technical references appropriate to its claims.
- A clear distinction from existing lessons.

Merge duplicates, split only when learning objectives genuinely differ, and mark supplementary topics that are not covered by the source curriculum.

## Content format mix

Use the format that best teaches the concept:

- Animated concept explainers.
- Practical screen-recorded demonstrations.
- Technical comparisons and decision guides.
- Career roadmaps and project walkthroughs.
- Quizzes, misconceptions, and revision lessons.

The mix of formats is adjustable based on audience response. Do not force every pillar into the same visual template.
