# TECHENIGMASTUDIOS Brand Guide

## Brand essence

**Decode. Understand. Build.**

TECHENIGMASTUDIOS makes complex technology understandable through accurate teaching, visual explanations, and practical demonstrations.

## Identity

| Attribute | Decision |
|---|---|
| Brand name | TECHENIGMASTUDIOS |
| Category | Technology education |
| Language | English |
| Presentation | Faceless |
| Core audience | Students, beginners, self-learners, aspiring technology professionals |
| Personality | Curious, clear, precise, confident, practical |
| Primary promise | Understand how technology works and what to learn or build next |

## Visual system

### Colours

Only the two approved brand colours should be used in core brand artwork:

- **Pure black:** `#000000`
- **Signal yellow:** `#FFD400`

Use yellow on black for primary text, diagrams, highlights, and motion graphics. Use black on yellow for high-emphasis cards and labels. Do not introduce unrelated accent colours, gradients, or decorative colour palettes.

### Typography

- Headlines: bold, modern sans-serif with strong legibility.
- Body and captions: clean sans-serif, comfortable at mobile size.
- Technical labels: consistent sizing and alignment.
- Keep wording concise; avoid dense paragraphs on screen.

Exact font families and logo construction remain open for a separate design decision.

## Faceless presentation rules

- Do not use the creator's face as the channel's presenter.
- Use narration, animated diagrams, screen recordings, typography, demonstrations, and purposeful motion graphics.
- A fictional mascot or recurring graphic character is optional and remains unapproved until separately designed.
- Do not use a realistic synthetic human presenter as the default identity.

## Video design principles

- Visuals must explain or reinforce the lesson; effects must not compete with it.
- Use motion to show relationships, sequences, transformations, and cause/effect.
- Prefer original diagrams and demonstrations over generic stock footage.
- Keep captions readable and synchronised with narration.
- Maintain safe margins for platform interface overlays.
- Use a consistent opening language and discreet brand signature, not a long intro.
- Make every video understandable without relying on colour alone; use labels, shapes, and positioning as well.

## Formats

1. Animated concept explainers.
2. Technical comparisons and decision guides.
3. Process, architecture, and algorithm visualisations.
4. Screen-recorded walkthroughs and practical demonstrations.
5. Career roadmaps, quizzes, and structured learning sequences.

## Audio identity

Use clear English narration with a consistent delivery style. The final choice between creator-recorded voice and AI narration should be tested with pilot videos. Keep music and sound design subordinate to speech and comprehension.

## Platform formats

- Short-form master: vertical 9:16, with a 1080 × 1920 working export target.
- Long-form master: horizontal 16:9.
- Create clean masters without platform watermarks; prepare platform-specific metadata separately.

## Non-negotiables

1. Faceless, English-language education.
2. Black `#000000` and yellow `#FFD400` identity.
3. Technical accuracy before virality.
4. Original explanations and practical value.
5. Consistency without repetitive templates.
6. Human review before publication.
