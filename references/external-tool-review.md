| [OpenCreator](https://github.com/krillinai/OpenCreator) | Codex runtime, approvals, task state and creative workspace architecture | Revisit later; not a current dependency |

## Excluded

Instagram engagement bots, unofficial private APIs and bulk scrapers are not part of TECHENIGMASTUDIOS. We will use public research signals, native platform tools and permitted official APIs. We will not automate likes/follows/comments/DMs, bypass rate limits, collect follower data or download third-party creator media for republishing.

See the canonical register for the complete list, including OpenSEO, WeKnora, OpenProject, FluxCD, Podcastfy, LangChain Social Media Agent, Ghost Editor, MotionClone, InstaPy, GramAddict, InstaTouch, instagrapi, InstaLooter and Claudegram.
